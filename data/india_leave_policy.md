---
title: India Leave and Benefits Policy
geo: India
department: HR
---
Employees based in India receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave requires manager approval and should normally be requested at least five working days in advance.

Eligible employees may receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in accordance with applicable Indian law and company policy. Employees should contact HR to confirm eligibility and documentation requirements.

India offices observe the published local holiday calendar. The calendar may differ by state and office location.
